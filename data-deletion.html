Delete Your Data

Last updated: 20 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your NodeTray account and the personal information linked to it at any time. This includes accounts created with email, Google or Facebook.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@nodetray.com with the subject “Delete my NodeTray account”.
  2. Write from the email address on your account, or tell us the email you used to sign in.
  3. We will delete your account and personal data within 30 days and confirm by email. We may keep limited records where the law requires it.

If you signed in with Facebook

You can also remove NodeTray from your Facebook account: go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find NodeTray and click Remove. Then email us so we can delete the data we hold.
