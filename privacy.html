Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information NodeTray (“we”, “us”) collects when you use nodetray.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Google sign-in: if you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Activity on the service: trades, loan requests and repayments you make through NodeTray.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information and IP address, used for security and to keep the service working.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in.
  • To provide trading, borrowing and repayment features.
  • To send important messages, such as email confirmation and password resets.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run NodeTray (for example, our hosting and authentication provider, Supabase) and when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed to meet legal and security requirements. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information by emailing us. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove NodeTray’s access in your Google Account settings.

Security

We use industry-standard protections, but no online service is completely secure. Please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
